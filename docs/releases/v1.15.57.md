# 1.15.57

Production-recall collection now persists a bounded `empty_reason` on zero-candidate pendings, excludes `session_deduped_prior_delivery` from `new_empty_count` (reported as `new_session_deduped_count`), and auto-review closes those cases with `session_deduped_prior_delivery` / `recall_status=not_evaluable` instead of `no_recall`. Existing session-dedupe pendings are reclassified from decision diagnostics at review time without rewriting store records. Session-dedupe policy, host-notification bypass, and Hermes session identity are unchanged.

This is a recall-attribution quality fix for empty production-recall pendings whose selector found evidence that session_dedupe then removed. It does not retire baseline high-water records, rewrite controlled production acceptance high-water, deploy, or claim the three nightly business gates are green.
